Frequently Asked Questions

Our kitchen handles dairy (cheddar, brioche buns, truffle butter, aioli), gluten (brioche buns, panko breadcrumbs), eggs (aioli, brioche), tree nuts (some desserts), and sesame seeds. While we take precautions, we cannot guarantee any item is completely allergen-free as all food is prepared in a shared kitchen.
Yes! We offer gluten-free seeded brioche buns upon request for any burger. We also have lettuce wrap / protein-style options for carb-free substitutions. Our fries are cooked in a dedicated gluten-free fryer. Please inform your server of any allergies so we can take extra precautions.
Absolutely! We offer dairy-free vegan cheese and aioli substitutions for any burger. OurLoaded Truffle Parmesan Fries can be made vegan with dairy-free cheese. We also have fresh salads and vegetable sides. Ask your server about our current vegan specials.
For parties of 8 or more, we recommend booking in advance through our booking page or by calling us at +1 (312) 555-0188. We have private booths, the Chef Counter Front, and the Patio Beer Garden available. For full venue buyouts or catering events, please contact our events team at events@burgerking-craft.com.
We understand plans change. You can cancel or modify your reservation up to 2 hours before your scheduled time at no charge. For no-shows or cancellations within 2 hours, a fee of $10 per guest may apply. For large group bookings (8+), we require 24 hours notice for cancellations.
Yes! Our executive pitmasters cater weddings, product launches, sports viewing parties, and corporate luncheons across the greater Chicago metropolitan area. We offer slider platters, finger foods, dessert bars, and full BBQ setups. Visit our Combos & Catering page or contact us for a custom quote.
We are open Monday through Thursday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Friday and Saturday from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time. Bar and shake station remain open until close.
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. For catering orders, we also accept bank transfers and corporate purchase orders. Split checks are available for parties of any size.
