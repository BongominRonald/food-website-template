The Signature Black Angus Truffle Double

$14.99

(4.9/5 from 420+ foodie reviews)

Two custom-ground 1/4 lb patties of dry-aged USDA Prime Angus beef, flame-seared over natural hickory coals, and glazed with imported black summer truffle butter.

Layered with double melted Wisconsin 18-month sharp white cheddar, crispy thick-cut applewood smoked bacon, balsamic caramelized sweet onions, organic baby arugula, and house-made roasted garlic truffle aioli on a daily butter-toasted brioche bun.

CALORIES 840 kcal
PROTEIN 52 g
PREP TIME 8–10 Mins
ORIGIN Midwest Angus
